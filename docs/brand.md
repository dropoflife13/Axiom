# Axiom brand mark

The Axiom mark is an original loop-and-gate construction:

- The loop is a 12-unit-radius circular stroke centered at `(16, 16)`.
- The loop has one intentional top gap. Its visible arc starts at approximately `(-110 degrees)` and ends at approximately `(-15 degrees)`.
- The verifier gate is a square-cornered bracket, 7.7 units wide and 2.75 units tall, closing the top gap.
- The lesson node is a solid circle of radius 2.05 units positioned on the upper-right stroke.
- The standard stroke is 2 units wide. The favicon uses 2.4 units for legibility at 16px.
- The mono variant uses `currentColor`. The duo variant uses violet `#a855f7` for the loop and cyan `#22d3ee` for the node.

Originality was not legally verified. A reverse-image search and trademark check are still required.
